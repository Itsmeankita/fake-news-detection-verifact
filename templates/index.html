Why it matters

Misinformation spreads faster than corrections.

Studies on online information spread consistently find that false stories reach more people, faster, than true ones — especially on social platforms where headlines are judged in seconds. A lightweight, transparent classifier like VeriFact can't replace human judgment or professional fact-checking, but it can act as a first-pass signal that prompts a second look before sharing.
